✓ Brand Do’s

  • • Always display the subtitle as MAKEOVER • STUDIO beneath MALAR’S.
  • • Use Royal Maroon (#4A0E17) + Imperial Gold (#D4AF37) on white, ivory, and light backgrounds.
  • • Maintain at least 1X clear space (equal to the gold tilak height) around all four sides of the lockup.
  • • Export 9:16 reels for Instagram Stories/Reels and 1:1 square frames for feed posts & DPs.

✕ Brand Don’ts

  • • Never render the logo in plain black or grey on white/ivory backgrounds—always use Royal Maroon & Gold.
  • • Never stretch, skew, or separate the 5 lotus petals and gold tilak jewel from the M-monogram proportions.
  • • Never place the logo over busy, low-contrast photographs without a dark maroon/noir overlay or translucent card.
  • • Never alter the letter-spacing or substitute fonts for MALAR’S or MAKEOVER • STUDIO.

# Malar's Makeover Studio — Complete Brand & Motion Suite

An interactive, self-contained HTML5 brand identity studio and motion graphics generator designed for **Malar's Makeover Studio**.

> **Live Demo:** [https://vaishnavkuttan.github.io/malars-makeover-brand-suite/](https://vaishnavkuttan.github.io/malars-makeover-brand-suite/)  
> *(Enable GitHub Pages on the `main` branch to activate this link.)*

---

## Brand Identity Overview

* **Brand Name:** Malar's Makeover Studio (*"Malar"* translates to *flower* in Tamil)
* **Tagline:** `MAKEOVER • STUDIO`
* **Emblem Anatomy:**
  * **Architectural "M" Monogram:** Symmetrical geometric peak representing structure, artistry, and precision.
  * **5-Petal Blooming Lotus:** Anchors the base of the monogram, celebrating timeless South Indian beauty and grace.
  * **Imperial Gold Tilak Jewel:** Positioned above the central vertex as a mark of auspiciousness and refinement.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Interactive Visual Studio
* **Live High-DPI Stage:** Tap anywhere on the stage to replay animations in real time.
* **4 Official Color Themes:**
  * **Royal Maroon** (`#4A0E17` background with Pearl Ivory & Imperial Gold)
  * **Bridal Ivory** (`#FAF6F0` background with Royal Maroon & Imperial Gold)
  * **Velvet Noir** (`#141011` background with Pearl Ivory & Imperial Gold)
  * **Transparent** (Checkerboard preview with Royal Maroon & Imperial Gold for clean overlays)
* **3 Signature Lockups:**
  * **Vertical Stack** (Primary emblem + stacked typography)
  * **Horizontal Banner** (Optically centered emblem, vertical gold rule, and wordmark)
  * **Icon Mark Only** (Standalone M-Lotus & Tilak emblem)

### 2. Six Motion Choreography Presets
1. **Organic Lotus Bounce:** Spring-arc petal burst with golden ripple ring, tilak drop, geometric **M** draw, and staggered wordmark reveal.
2. **Sketch-to-Grid Prism:** Hand-sketched ivory paper outline $\rightarrow$ dark architectural blueprint grid with golden ratio circles $\rightarrow$ extruded 3D metallic gold bevel $\rightarrow$ signature flourish.
3. **Precision Guide & Brand Showcase:** Sweeping diagonal and radial construction beams $\rightarrow$ rapid brand-world cuts (velvet foil box, silk ivory card, gold medallion) $\rightarrow$ crisp primary lockup.
4. **Luxury Reveal:** Classic sequential stroke and wordmark entrance.
5. **Royal Shimmer:** Soft radial gold aura pulse across the emblem.
6. **Soft Bloom:** Gentle scale and opacity blossom.

### 3. One-Click Asset & HD Reel Exporter
* **Vector & Raster Exports:** Download theme-synced **SVG**, high-resolution **PNG**, and **1:1 Social DP** files.
* **Built-in HD Video Recorder (`MediaRecorder`):** Export ready-to-post motion reels in **9:16** (Instagram Reels / Stories), **1:1** (Square Feed), or **16:9** (YouTube / Widescreen) across **3.0s**, **4.5s**, or **6.5s** durations.

### 4. Full Brand Kit Manual
* **Golden Ratio Construction Blueprint:** `1.618:1` guide rings, `1X` clear-space boundaries, and minimum sizing specs.
* **Social Avatar & Frame Specimens:** Ready-to-download `1080×1080` Square Post Frames and `1000×1000` Circular Social DPs.
* **Official Color System & Usage Ratio:** Click-to-copy HEX/RGB swatches with the **55% / 30% / 15%** visual color proportion bar.
* **Typography Hierarchy:** Specimens for **Cinzel**, **Montserrat**, and **Cormorant Garamond**.
* **Brand Applications & Usage Rules:** Miniature mockups and clear Do's & Don'ts.

## Quick Start & Hosting

No build tools, npm packages, or external frameworks are required—everything runs natively inside a single self-contained HTML file (`index.html` / `malars-makeover-complete-suite.html`).

1. **Run Locally:** Download `index.html` and open it directly in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.
2. **Host on GitHub Pages:**
   * Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Pages** in this repository.
   * Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
   * Choose the **`main`** branch and **`/ (root)`** folder, then click **Save**.
